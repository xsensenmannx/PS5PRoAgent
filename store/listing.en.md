# Console Radar – Price Alerts

## Short description (max. 80 characters)

Stock, prices and alerts for the PS5 Pro 2 TB – verified, no account.

## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

Console Radar watches 42 electronics retailers in Germany and Austria and shows
you where the PlayStation 5 Pro 2 TB is available – and at what price.

No scrolling through 42 tabs, no account, no ads. At a glance you see who has it,
what it costs, and whether the price moved since the last scan.

**What the app does**

• Radar overview of all retailers, hit list with price and status
• Price history per retailer, 30-day low and change since the last scan
• Hit verification: every hit is checked a second time. Cards show "verified",
  "unverified" or "deviation" – no alert based on a stale page.
• Alerts as Android notifications, optionally via webhook to ntfy, Telegram or
  Discord
• Alert rules: in stock only, all-time lows only, quiet hours, mute retailers,
  your own price limit
• Fast mode: checks the six key retailers every minute while the app is open
• Background scan with a visible notification, even with the screen off
• Price and availability history stays on your device

**What the app does not do**

• It does not buy anything or fill a cart.
• It does not solve CAPTCHAs or bypass bot protection.
• It does not create retailer accounts and does not send data to us.
• It does not guarantee availability. Retailers change stock and prices at any
  time; the app shows what it read at scan time.

**Privacy**

No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, no tracking. Settings and history stay on
the device. Only if you configure a webhook does the app send the hit to the
endpoint you chose. See the privacy policy for details.
